⚓ Before you buy for a Léman mooring in Geneva
  • Boat licence: on Swiss waters a licence is needed above 6 kW engine power or 15 m² of sail; on Lake Geneva the motor limit is 10 CV. Category A = motor (18+), D = sail (14+). OFT · ge.ch
  • Plates: a boat moored in a Geneva port must be registered in Geneva (OCV, CHF 105). Moving a boat from another canton or changing owner means a new navigation permit. ge.ch: immatriculer un bateau
  • Inspection: required before registration for motorboats over 9 years old last checked more than 4 years ago (sail and rowing boats: more than 7 years). Ask the seller for the permit and the last inspection date.
  • Liability insurance is required to register, except rowing boats and sailboats up to 15 m² of sail.
  • Boats far from the Léman need road transport: check for a trailer, and the beam and weight for your slip.
