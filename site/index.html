⚓ Before you buy for a Léman mooring in Geneva

  • Licence needed above 6 kW engine or 15 m² sail on Swiss waters; on Lake Geneva the motor limit is 10 CV (OFT, ge.ch).
  • A boat kept in a Geneva port is registered in Geneva (OCV, CHF 105); a change of canton or owner needs a new navigation permit (ge.ch).
  • Technical inspection before registration for motorboats over 9 years old last checked more than 4 years ago (sail/rowing: more than 7 years).
